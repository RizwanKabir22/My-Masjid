My Masjid Resource Management

A strategic product assessment showing why My Masjid should expand beyond prayer times and announcements into a mosque-native operating layer for money, people, governance, programs, and community capacity.

Research & analysis by Rizwan Kabir Khan

Executive Report
Market verdict

The strongest opportunity is resource management.

The research suggests the next major My Masjid opportunity is not another prayer-times or announcement layer. The bigger gap is a mosque-native operating system that helps masjids manage funds, volunteers, households, committees, programs, and community capacity in one place.

134
Biggest gap

Mosque software is still split between front door and back office.

Mosque-native tools are strongest at prayer schedules, screens, events, and communication. Church and nonprofit products are stronger in donor records, volunteers, workflows, and reporting, but they are not built around zakat, sadaqah, Ramadan surges, mosque committees, or multilingual Muslim communities.

2101213
Strategic opening

My Masjid can connect what others keep separate.

You already have worshipper touchpoints and mosque communication surfaces. If resource management is added carefully, My Masjid can connect announcements, donation campaigns, volunteer asks, class registrations, committee tasks, and donor follow-up into one operating layer.

What mosques truly need

Money, people, roles, and decisions need one system.

1A unified household and community graph so one person can be a donor, volunteer, parent, student, teacher, business owner, or committee member.
2An Islamic giving ledger for zakat, sadaqah, fitrah, waqf, designated funds, donor intent, receipts, and campaign attribution.
3Volunteer and committee workflows built around real mosque governance, not generic shift scheduling only.
4Program, class, sponsorship, and community resource tools that convert mosque communication into measurable action.
1101115
Risks to avoid

Do not build donations as an isolated feature.

The risk is creating one more disconnected module. The real product value comes from connecting donor data, fund rules, campaign surfaces, volunteer activity, event attendance, committee work, and community skills into one reliable resource graph.

Generic CRM features will not feel mosque-native enough.
Too much accounting depth too early can slow the MVP.
Payment hardware, tax receipts, and charity compliance will vary by country.

Final recommendation
Build My Masjid as the operating system for mosques worldwide.

The best direction is not to compete only on prayer screens, announcements, or donations. The strongest path is a connected resource management layer that helps a masjid understand who gives, who serves, who decides, who attends, who can help, and what the community can do together.
